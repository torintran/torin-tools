# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Give feedback on the work, make room for different experience levels, and keep discussions relevant to the project.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and sharing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community.

Use GitHub's reporting controls for abusive content or accounts. Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md) for private vulnerability disclosure.
